# Student guide

1. Open ProctorLAN and choose **I'm a Student → Join Exam**.
2. Enter what your teacher shows on screen:
   - **Teacher's address**, like `192.168.1.20:38123`
   - **Session code**, 5 characters
   - **Full name** and **Student ID**
3. Press **Join exam**. You land in the waiting room until the teacher starts.

## If your connection drops
The app reconnects by itself (a banner says so) and your place is saved. If the app closes, open the Join screen again and press **Rejoin**. You return to the same attempt.

## If "already joined" appears
Someone already used that student ID in this session. If it was you on another device or you reinstalled, ask the teacher to **Remove** your entry in the session screen, then join again.

## If it cannot connect
- Check you are on the same Wi-Fi or network as the teacher's computer.
- Check the address matches the teacher's screen exactly, and the session is open.
- Teacher: Windows may have blocked ProctorLAN in the firewall. Allow it on **Private networks**.

## During the exam
- One question at a time. Your answers save automatically; the badge at the top shows "All answers saved".
- The clock is the teacher's computer's clock. It turns amber at 5 minutes and red at 1 minute.
- If the teacher pauses, your screen locks and the clock stops until they resume.
- Press **Submit exam** when finished. A dialog lists any questions you left blank. After submitting you cannot change answers.
- If time runs out the exam may submit for you automatically.
- If your connection drops, keep working: answers are kept on your computer (even if the app closes) and sent when you reconnect. Before you submit, make sure the badge says saved.
- If you see "closed because this exam was opened on another device or window", only one window can hold your exam at a time. Use the one that is still open.

## What your teacher can see

During the exam your teacher is told if you leave the exam window, switch tabs, or lose your connection, and how long you were away. Nothing is recorded before the exam opens or after you submit. Stay on the exam screen until you press Submit.
